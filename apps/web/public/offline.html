Offline support

You deserve help, even when you’re offline.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger: contact the emergency service for your location, or go to a nearby emergency department if you can. This app is not monitored and cannot dispatch help.

If it feels safe to do so

  • Move toward a public or safer place, if that is possible for you.
  • Contact someone you trust and tell them what would help right now.
  • Use a local crisis or specialist support service. If you have no data connection, try a phone call or text if those are available to you.
  • Take one small step: notice your surroundings, feel your feet on the floor, or pause and breathe at your own pace.

Find a crisis service when you’re back online

  • Find A Helpline — crisis support directories by country.
  • International Association for Suicide Prevention — crisis-centre resources.
  • 988 Lifeline — United States support information.

Services and availability differ by country. These links need an internet connection. This app does not collect your location, provide emergency monitoring, or replace licensed therapy or emergency services.
